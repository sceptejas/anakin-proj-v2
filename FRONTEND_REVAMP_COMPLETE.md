Complete UI redesign inspired by Optimus/Vercel aesthetic while keeping **ALL backend logic 100% intact**.

### Design Transformation

**Before (Cyber-Noir):**
- Black background (#000000)
- Neon green text (#00FF41)
- Terminal/hacker aesthetic
- Glitch effects
- Monospace font

**After (Modern Clean):**
- Light background (#fafafa)
- Black text with gray accents
- Professional, minimal design
- Smooth animations

## 🎯 Design Principles

### Typography
- **Headings**: 5xl-7xl, font-normal (not bold)
- **Body**: text-lg, text-gray-600/700
- **Labels**: text-sm, font-medium
- **System font stack** (Apple/Segoe UI)

### Colors
- **Background**: #fafafa (off-white)
- **Cards**: white with border-gray-200
- **Text**: black, gray-600, gray-700
- **Accents**: 
  - Success: #10b981 (green)
  - Warning: #f59e0b (yellow)
  - Error: #ef4444 (red)
- **Primary**: black

### Spacing
- **Padding**: p-6, p-8 (cards)
- **Gaps**: gap-4, gap-6, gap-8
- **Margins**: mb-4, mb-6, mb-8, mb-12

### Borders
- **Radius**: rounded-lg (8px), rounded-2xl (16px)
- **Width**: border (1px), border-2 (2px)
- **Color**: border-gray-200, border-red-200, border-green-200

### Shadows
- **Cards**: shadow-sm (subtle)
- **Verdict**: shadow-lg (prominent)
